How group departures work

What is included in a Rihla group departure?

Every scheduled departure flies from your home city with a Rihla tour leader and a scholar. Groups are capped at 35 pilgrims, and the price includes flights, the Saudi visa, hotels, transfers and ziyarah.

i.

Fly together

Your tour leader meets the group at the departure airport and stays with you until you land home.

ii.

A scholar with you

Pre-departure classes, then guidance at the miqat, during tawaf and sa'i, and daily reminders.

iii.

Small groups

Capped at 35 pilgrims, or 12 on Elite departures, so everyone is looked after by name.

iv.

Your own dates

Families and masjid groups of 15 or more can request a private departure on any dates.
