Day by day

What does a 12-night Signature Umrah look like?

Most pilgrims perform Umrah on the first night in Makkah with their scholar, spend six more days in prayer and ziyarah near the Haram, then take the Haramain high-speed train to Madinah for five nights at the Prophet's Mosque ﷺ.

  1. Day 1

    Depart & enter ihram

    Fly from your home city. Your scholar announces the miqat on board; arrive in Jeddah and transfer privately to Makkah.

  2. Day 1–2

    Perform Umrah together

    Guided tawaf, sa'i and halq or taqsir with your scholar, then rest close to the Haram.

  3. Day 3–7

    Makkah

    Pray every salah in the Haram, attend daily reminders, and visit Jabal al-Nour, Thawr, Arafah, Muzdalifah and Mina.

  4. Day 8

    Haramain train to Madinah

    Ride the high-speed train in business class — about two and a half hours.

  5. Day 8–12

    Madinah

    Send salam at the Prophet's Mosque ﷺ, visit the Rawdah with a Nusuk permit, and see Quba, Uhud and Qiblatayn.

  6. Day 13

    Return home

    Private transfer to Madinah airport and your flight home, with Zamzam water in your luggage.
