How much does Umrah cost from the UK or USA in 2027?

A 10–14 night Umrah package costs roughly $1,900–$2,600 with 4★ hotels, $3,200–$4,800 with 5★ hotels within 300 m of the Haram, and $5,400 or more for Ramadan and private journeys. Rihla packages include return flights, the Saudi visa, hotels, transfers and guided ziyarah.

Can I perform Umrah on a Saudi tourist visa?

Yes. Citizens of the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and most European countries can apply for a Saudi tourist e-visa online and perform Umrah outside the Hajj period. Rihla files the visa application for you as part of every package.

Can women travel for Umrah without a mahram?

Saudi regulations currently allow women to perform Umrah without a mahram, and Rihla runs women-only groups led by a female scholar. Scholarly opinions differ on this question, so we encourage you to follow the guidance you trust; our scholars are happy to discuss it.

How far are your hotels from the Haram?

Essentials hotels are about 600 m from Masjid al-Haram, Signature hotels about 150 m, and Elite hotels about 50 m with Haram-view rooms. In Madinah, every tier stays within 300 m of Masjid an-Nabawi.

What is included in a Rihla Umrah package?

Every package includes return flights, the Saudi visa, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, airport and intercity transfers, guided ziyarah, a travelling scholar and our 24/7 ground team. Signature and Elite add the Haramain train in business class and more meals.
